# 🏛️ Bio-ML Agent Brain Architecture Principles

These 5 pillars form the "Spine" of the Phase R6 Unified Research Operating System.

## 1. "One mission, one truth."
Every research mission must have a single, canonical state. There are no fragmented or conflicting realities; the `ProjectState` and `MissionPlan` are the absolute authorities.

## 2. "Artifacts are first-class citizens."
Outputs are not just files; they are identifiable, traceable, and versioned entities. Every insight must be linked to its evidence via the `ArtifactGraph`.

## 3. "Agents must be replaceable, system must remain stable."
The system relies on the **Unified Agent Contract**, not individual model quirks. Swapping an LLM or a specialized agent must not break the orchestration DAG.

## 4. "Failures are normal; recovery is a feature."
Scientific tasks are complex and long-running. The system assumes transient failures will occur and provides `RecoveryManager` and `CheckpointStore` as core features, not afterthoughts.

## 5. "Integration quality matters as much as model quality."
A powerful model is only as effective as its integration. The platform prioritizes system-level consistency, governance, and observability to ensure models provide professional-grade scientific value.

---
**Core Identity**: Not just a collection of agents, but a **Mission-Scenario-Based, Centrally-Orchestrated Scientific Platform.**
